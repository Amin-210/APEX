# Market profile (example): Kenya

An example market profile. A store profile points to it with `market profile: market-example-kenya.md`.
For another market, copy this file's shape (places, everyday words, moments, money, scenes) and fill
it for that market. Use these elements to make review language and copy feel authentic to the market.

## Place names (use in reviews)

**Major cities:**
- Nairobi (capital, urban professional)
- Mombasa (coast, hot/humid, beach)
- Kisumu (lake region)
- Nakuru (smaller city, growing)
- Eldoret (rural-ish, athletics)
- Thika (industrial, near Nairobi)

**Nairobi neighborhoods (more specific):**
- Westlands (upscale, expats, business)
- Karen (suburban, wealthy)
- Lavington (residential, family)
- Kileleshwa (residential)
- Ruaka, Kiambu, Kikuyu (suburban, rising middle class)
- South B, South C, Embakasi (working class)
- CBD, Kilimani (offices)

**Other:**
- Naivasha, Nyeri, Meru, Machakos, Kakamega (regional towns)

## Slang / casual expressions

- **Shaggs** = village / rural home (going "to shaggs" = trip upcountry)
- **Mama Fua** = cleaning lady, house helper
- **Boda boda** = motorcycle taxi
- **Matatu** = minibus public transport
- **Duka** = small shop / corner store
- **Asante / Asante sana** = thank you / thank you very much (use sparingly, feels authentic)
- **Sawa** = OK / cool (use very sparingly)
- **Pesa** = money

## Money references

- **Always KSh** (not USD, not "shillings" — Kenyans say "KSh")
- Common price comparisons:
  - Car wash: KSh 300-500
  - Toilet brush: KSh 200-500
  - Cheap Chinese tool: KSh 1,500-2,500
  - Mid-range tool: KSh 3,500-5,000
  - Professional CCTV install: KSh 30,000+
  - Dermatologist visit: KSh 3,000-8,000
- Phrasings:
  - "I used to pay KSh 300 every week for a car wash"
  - "This paid for itself in 3 months"
  - "I have spent over KSh 30,000 on dermatologists"
  - "Worth every shilling"

## Common 4-star review patterns (the 12% honest ones)

- "Works well, just wish the cable was a bit longer"
- "Solid product but took me a moment to figure out the velcro"
- "Great quality but the adhesive could be stronger"
- "Does the job, would have liked a slightly bigger screen"
- "Works as described — just remember you need to buy a memory card separately"
- "Strong jet but depends on your tap pressure"
- "Good product, the cable could be longer but I use a power bank instead"

## Sample review tone (mix these patterns)

**Short factual (5★):** "Delivery to Mombasa was fast. The fan is strong and looks premium. Very satisfied."

**Story-based (5★):** "My mum is 68 with bad knees. She can finally clean her bathroom without my help. I bought her one and now my sister is asking where to buy."

**Money-focused (5★):** "I used to pay KSh 300 every week for a car wash. With this nozzle I wash at home in 20 minutes. Paid for itself in 3 months."

**Persona-specific (5★):** "I drive Uber and my passengers always comment how comfortable the back seat is. My ratings have improved. Worth every shilling."

**4★ honest:** "Works very well, the jet is strong. Just wish the adapter was slightly tighter on my hose but otherwise excellent."

## Things to AVOID in reviews

- Sounding too American or too British
- Overusing slang (1-2 sparingly per review batch)
- Fake-sounding superlatives like "AMAZING!!!" or "10/10"
- Specific medical claims even in reviews ("cured my eczema")
- Comparing to specific brand competitors by name
- Anything political, tribal, or controversial
