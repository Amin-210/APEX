# Copy Voice Check

persuasion-check asks whether the beats exist. This asks whether the words land. A page can pass every structural test and still read like a well-reasoned document about a dish rack, while the map says she is buying the end of a daily defeat.

Runs AFTER persuasion-check, BEFORE the accent decision and the build.

## Inputs
The finished copy in page order, the customer mind map (layers 2, 3, 4, 6, 7 and the language bank), and the persuasion-check scorecard.
No map → stop and ask. Without the language bank this becomes a style opinion.

## Output, in this order

1. **The line-by-line read** — each section: the line that works, the line that does not, and why, with the map line behind the verdict.
2. **The scorecard** — the 12 tests below, PASS / WEAK / FAIL.
3. **The quoted weak lines with their rewrites** — old line, new line, one reason.
4. **The rewritten sections in full**, ready to paste. Same sections, same order, same design classes: only the words change.

## The 12 voice tests

| # | Test | FAIL when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Second person** | A section runs with the product as the subject throughout ("the tray collects the water") instead of her ("you empty it once a week") |
| 2 | **Her exact words** | A section contains none of the language bank - the copy is written in brand English, not in hers |
| 3 | **Scene, not list** | The pain point is a checklist of problems instead of one moment she can picture. Lists are for proof; pain needs a scene |
| 4 | **Headings contain a person or a consequence** | A heading is a spec summary ("Two Tiers In The Space Of One Rack") instead of what it means for her ("Your sufurias finally have somewhere to go") |
| 5 | **Sensory detail** | Nothing in the page can be heard, smelled, touched or felt - only seen and reasoned |
| 6 | **The hook is a moment** | The hook states a benefit or a state she has to translate, instead of the moment the ad promised her |
| 7 | **One idea per sentence** | Sentences carry two claims joined by "and" or a comma, so neither lands |
| 8 | **Specificity** | Adjectives where numbers, times, places or names belong ("very durable" instead of "six weeks of daily washing") |
| 9 | **Rhythm and length** | Every sentence is the same length; no short line lands the point. A 3-5 word sentence after two long ones is what makes a page feel written |
| 10 | **The reframe leads its block** | A section buries its strongest line at the end, where most readers never arrive |
| 11 | **Repetition without insistence** | The same promise is repeated more than three times in the same words; the third reads as insistence, not reassurance |
| 12 | **Last line of every section** | A section ends on a fact or a spec instead of a consequence, a feeling or her |

## Scoring notes
- Quote the line. A verdict without the line quoted is unusable.
- Every rewrite must be traceable to the map: her word, her moment, her fear. Never a better-sounding invention.
- Never rewrite a line that passes. Improving good copy costs the page its voice consistency.
- Rewrites respect the word budgets already set by the structure (headings, subtitles, section lengths) and the design classes. Words only.
- Claims cannot change. If a rewrite would make a claim stronger than the product can keep, keep the weaker line and say why.

## Working with the other skills

| Skill | Relationship |
|---|---|
| customer-mind-map | The only source for rewrites: layers 2, 3, 4, 6, 7 and the language bank |
| persuasion-check | Runs first. It fixes what is missing; this fixes how what is there is written |
| design-system | Untouched. Same sections, same classes, same word budgets |
| review-frames | Not audited here - review text is a real customer's words and is never rewritten for voice, only lightly edited |
| compliance-rules | Outrank every rewrite. A more vivid line that overclaims is rejected |

## What this skill does not do
It does not add, remove, move or merge sections - that is persuasion-check.
It does not touch design, colour, layout or the build.
It does not rewrite customer reviews.
